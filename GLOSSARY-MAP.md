# Glossary map

## Contexts

- [World and consequence presentation](GLOSSARY.md): Players, admission, observations and world-native consequences.
- [Release operations](docs/release/GLOSSARY.md): review, preview authorization and landing authority.

## Relationships

- World consequence compatibility is not release approval or hosted acceptance.
- Review readiness is separate from preview authorization; merge eligibility is separate from deployment authority.

See [review/resource boundaries](docs/REVIEW-RESOURCE-BOUNDARIES.md) and [WAIT compatibility](docs/WAIT-OUTCOME-COMPATIBILITY-850.md) for the adopted decisions. Glossaries define language; those documents hold the operational and presentation contracts.
