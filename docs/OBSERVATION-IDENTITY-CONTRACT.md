## Settled scope and precise fields contract

A new `MESSAGE` or `TRADE` suggestion in `Observation.affordances` may target a Player only when the observer and candidate are co-located. At `deriveAffordances` (`workers/noema/src/actions.ts:3172-3191,3325-3356`), require a nonempty `input.roomId` and exact `p.room_id === input.roomId`; exclude `p.player_id === selfId`. A missing observer `roomId`, candidate `room_id`, or mismatch produces **no new MESSAGE/TRADE suggestion**, even for a previously known distant counterparty. This applies to both actions' `target_id`, `target_label`, `label`, and generated `cmd`; it does not remove all-world `otherPlayers` before other affordance loops. An explicit distant MESSAGE may still address an existing Player by literal full player ID or exact handle under the established recipient rules; existing exact distant actions and their admission rules are not changed here. The observer's own inbox and trades, including prior counterparties and payloads, remain available under their existing viewer-specific projections. No canonical world-state, event, private message payload, or organization schema mutation belongs to this response-projection slice.

`applyWorldCommand(w, principal, envelope, settle, opts)` (`workers/noema/src/world-actions.ts:1987-1993`) is the existing local observation seam. A two-room reducer fixture can inspect a `LOOK` observation and then submit an explicit distant MESSAGE; it cannot prove hosted persistence, isolation, or role revocation. `buildObservation` supplies entered Players with `player_id`, `handle`, `room_id`, `practice`, then calls `deriveAffordances` with `roomId: room.room_id` (`world-actions.ts:896-950`). HELP separately calls it with `roomId: room?.room_id` (`world-actions.ts:2240-2283`), so absent room identity must fail closed for new MESSAGE/TRADE targets there too. HELP's candidate source includes every `w.players` entry without the ordinary entered filter (`:2253-2258`); the co-location predicate bounds the new social suggestions but does not settle that wider discrepancy for other affordances.

## #793 bounded extension (operator-approved; local only)

The preceding undecided rows and historical slice-9 limits describe the prior decision state, not the current operator rule. In `applyWorldCommand` LOOK and developer HELP observations, every ACTIVE organization retains its nonidentity summary. Only an **actual current member** sees `creator_id` and full `members[]` IDs/roles; outsiders receive `members: []` and no `creator_id`, even if they founded the organization or a member is co-located. `players_here` remains independently co-located. Organization canonical records and ownership grants are unchanged.

`ORG_INVITE` and `BUILD.SHARE` unsolicited targets require either a candidate in the same nonempty room as the observer or a current member of **an organization the observer actually belongs to**. Office assignment, succession designation and consent keep their additional action-specific membership, officer, vacancy and track predicates. HELP uses entered players for its candidate source, like LOOK. MESSAGE/TRADE still use only their stricter co-location predicate. Explicit distant authorized actions, viewer-held messages/trades and replay remain separate from unsolicited suggestions.

Specs `Noema-Specs@b86c4b09cf75b3027d87998eff0952c2421bf52d:docs/GC4-S1-OFFICES.md:107-117` authorizes a public/**permissioned** institution view showing name, occupied/vacant state, holder **handle**, and public profile id. The operator has now authorized every entered Player's PLAY organization view as that public institution view, including remote organizations. Outsiders receive only seat metadata and the current entered holder's verified public handle in structured offices, `office_lines`, and PLAY text; they never receive `holder_player_id` or any successor identity. Absent, unentered, minted, ID-shaped, operator/smoke, room-impersonating, or unsafe source handles yield generic `occupied` instead of an ID-derived alias. Own members keep their canonical roster and office-holder IDs for authorized office actions; narration never falls back from a missing holder or successor handle to an ID-derived name. The existing member-only succession view remains separate from global public entitlement. No org state, grants, or action admission changes.
