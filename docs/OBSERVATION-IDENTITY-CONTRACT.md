## Settled scope and precise fields contract

A new `MESSAGE` or `TRADE` suggestion in `Observation.affordances` may target a Player only when the observer and candidate are co-located. At `deriveAffordances` (`workers/noema/src/actions.ts:3172-3191,3325-3356`), require a nonempty `input.roomId` and exact `p.room_id === input.roomId`; exclude `p.player_id === selfId`. A missing observer `roomId`, candidate `room_id`, or mismatch produces **no new MESSAGE/TRADE suggestion**, even for a previously known distant counterparty. This applies to both actions' `target_id`, `target_label`, `label`, and generated `cmd`; it does not remove all-world `otherPlayers` before other affordance loops. An explicit distant MESSAGE may still address an existing Player by literal full player ID or exact handle under the established recipient rules; existing exact distant actions and their admission rules are not changed here. The observer's own inbox and trades, including prior counterparties and payloads, remain available under their existing viewer-specific projections. No canonical world-state, event, private message payload, or organization schema mutation belongs to this response-projection slice.

`applyWorldCommand(w, principal, envelope, settle, opts)` (`workers/noema/src/world-actions.ts:1987-1993`) is the existing local observation seam. A two-room reducer fixture can inspect a `LOOK` observation and then submit an explicit distant MESSAGE; it cannot prove hosted persistence, isolation, or role revocation. `buildObservation` supplies entered Players with `player_id`, `handle`, `room_id`, `practice`, then calls `deriveAffordances` with `roomId: room.room_id` (`world-actions.ts:896-950`). HELP separately calls it with `roomId: room?.room_id` (`world-actions.ts:2240-2283`), so absent room identity must fail closed for new MESSAGE/TRADE targets there too. HELP's candidate source includes every `w.players` entry without the ordinary entered filter (`:2253-2258`); the co-location predicate bounds the new social suggestions but does not settle that wider discrepancy for other affordances.
