A short map of what the runtime is allowed to be.

This is not a feature list. It is a reading of the frozen contract: Noema-Specs as implemented by Noema Runtime. The product UI milestone may change how humans see the world; it must not change what the world is.

Canonicalization is noema-jcs/1. Event catalog event-catalog/0.1. Claim labels remain OBSERVED, INFERRED, SPECULATIVE, and NOT_COMPUTABLE. There are no consciousness scores.

The loop

Players and agents act in a durable chamber: enter, look, move, inspect, message, wait. Those acts append world events under one fenced writer. After the ledger commits, optional research may capture trajectories. Frontier may raise pressure only by injecting ledger events through reducers. Observatory may detect. Lab may fork. Compiler may capture a test when Lab is READY. LEARN may rebuild a capability graph as a disposable index. Deep Time may hold scars and institutions as history, not as a second truth. Genesis may create a world only as ADMIN, then freezes.

The loop is closed on purpose: PLAY → NOTICE → TEST → CAPTURE → LEARN. Nothing in that sequence rewrites history after the fact.

Where state lives

Local PLAY uses SQLite, including :memory:, with exclusive write transactions. Production-shaped deploys use PostgreSQL. Cycle batches commit under SERIALIZABLE isolation with an expected revision and a process writer fence. Partial canonical commits are forbidden. Research tables are rebuildable; they are not the ledger.

On restart the world rehydrates from seed plus events. Application lifecycle and world lifecycle stay separate — a deploy must not reset economy, factions, or sequence by accident.

Operator surface

noema-verify runs the OPERATIONS checklist and prints NOEMA VERIFY: PASS or fails closed. noema-backup emits a portable bundle without secrets. noema-restore installs into a clean store with a fresh writer fence. For research-isolated export, signed receipts are mandatory: missing or invalid signatures are INVALID_EVIDENCE, never silently unsigned evidence. Resume and acknowledgement windows are bounded and may only reference committed sequences. 

# golden path shape
docker compose up
noema-verify --db … --seed fixtures/v01-seed/world-seed.json
noema-export-evidence --profile research-isolated --keyring var/evidence-keyring.json

Deferred on purpose: v0.8 Phenomena, graph databases as required infra, microservices, LLM claim-bearing planners, rich product UI, asymmetric public evidence keys. Those need explicit Specs or product work — not silent runtime invention.

Gateway the UI should treat as law

Health, ready, version, manifest, and config are public JSON. Watch live is redacted. Play mutates through sessions. Research routes require RESEARCHER or ADMIN. Genesis requires ADMIN. Errors carry stable codes. Ready means the world can play — not that every research worker is green.

If the interface needs a second ledger to feel correct, the interface is wrong.

Invariants worth printing once

One writer. Research is not truth. Frontier uses the ledger. Lab does not touch production. CAPTURE waits for READY. Genesis freezes after activate. Lore does not outrank evidence. Secrets stay out of public bundles. Invalid export receipts fail closed. No consciousness scores — ever.

For the tour of surfaces rather than the memo, use the workbench. For normative text, open Specs. For the pin machine reads, see spec-compat.json.
